## Explicit exclusions

- The [July 13 $100 Codex-credit promotion](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2076495156757577895)
  is ordinary promotional usage credit, not a hard reset or banked reset coupon.
- The [July 26 "Trying, but not sure this time" reply](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2081446159361675631)
  concerns a requested **Claude** reset, not a Codex/ChatGPT Work grant.
- The [July 26 "get back to Work" reply](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2081447286782218501)
  is a joke and contains no reset commitment.
- A hard reset and a banked reset are different mechanisms. A hard reset
  rewrites active quota windows; a banked reset is a storable, user-redeemable
  benefit.

## Authoritative cross-checks

- OpenAI's [ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)
  document reset banking, the launch reset, referral grants, and a 30-day
  usability period for banked resets granted under that offer.
- OpenAI's current [Codex Referral Promotions terms](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001271-codex-referral-promotions)
  say a banked Codex rate-limit reset must be used within 30 days unless the
  particular offer says otherwise. This policy does **not** prove that every
  bulk/milestone coupon has the same expiry; the offer shown on the account is
  controlling.
- The official [OpenAI Status incident](https://status.openai.com/incidents/01KW2E6W0503W4NXJNCVAG8V6T)
  corroborates the June 26-29 investigation into unexpectedly fast usage-limit
  depletion and identifies incorrect abuse/fraud rate limiting for some
  accounts.
